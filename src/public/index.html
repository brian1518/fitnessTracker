Privacy summary

A plain description of what this application stores and where. It is not a legal certification.

What is stored in your account

  • Your name and email address, and a salted hash of your password.
  • Workouts, sets, routines, body measurements, goals, your training profile, and saved coach answers.
  • Session records used to keep you signed in. They expire after seven days and are removed when you sign out.

Account data is kept in the application's database on its hosting provider. Only your own signed-in account can read it through the application.

What stays on your device

  • A workout in progress is kept in this browser's storage until you finish or discard it. It is not in your account until you finish.
  • On the public website, a sign-in token is kept for the current browser tab.

AI coach

AI interpretation is off unless the server operator has enabled it and you have turned on consent in Profile. When both are true and you ask the coach or generate a routine, a summary of your recent training and the coaching details in your profile are sent to xAI to produce the answer. Your name and email are not included. When AI is not used, answers come from built-in rules and are labelled that way.

What is not done

The application loads no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts.

Your controls

  • Export: Profile → Account → Export my data downloads everything in your account as a JSON file.
  • Delete: Profile → Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and all records linked to it.
  • AI consent: Profile → AI coach. You can withdraw it at any time.

Help

How saving works

While you log, your workout is a draft on this device. Selecting Finish workout saves the sets you have checked off to your account; unchecked sets are left out, and you are asked before that happens.

Units

Weights are in kilograms and body measurements in centimetres.

Forgotten password

Password reset by email is not available yet because the application does not send email. If you are locked out, use the contact link below.

Slow first load

The server sleeps when idle. The first request afterwards can take up to a minute. This deployment runs on trial hosting, so export your data from time to time.

Training guidance

Suggestions are general training guidance. They are not medical advice and cannot diagnose or treat pain or injury.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question on the project's issue tracker (opens GitHub). Do not post passwords or personal details there.
